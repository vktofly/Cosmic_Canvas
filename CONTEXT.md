# Context & Domain Glossary (Cosmic Canvas)

## Core Domain Terms

- **Event Horizon**: The boundary defining the region of space around a black hole from which nothing, not even light, can escape.
- **Accretion Disk**: A structure formed by diffuse material in orbital motion around a massive central body (black hole).
- **Gravitational Lensing**: The distortion of background light caused by the gravitational field of the black hole.
- **Ray Marching**: An algorithmic rendering technique used in shaders to trace paths of light rays through curved spacetime.
- **Observer Camera**: The virtual viewpoint in the 3D space allowing the user to rotate, zoom, and inspect cosmic phenomena.
- **Schwarzschild Radius ($R_s$)**: The physical radius defining the size of the Event Horizon based on black hole mass.
- **Photon Sphere**: The spherical region where gravity is strong enough that light travels in unstable circular orbits.
- **Doppler Beaming**: Asymmetric brightening of the accretion disk caused by relativistic movement toward the observer.
- **Shifting Vector**: Real-time parameter inputs (Mass, Spin, Tilt Angle, Camera Distance) that mutate the simulation geometry.
