# 10x Product Opportunities: Gallery Page Power Features

**Session Target**: Spacetime Preset Gallery (`/gallery.html`)  
**Date**: 2026-08-14  
**Author**: Product Strategist  

---

## 1. The Core 10x Moves

### 🌌 1. Live Interactive WebGL Micro-Canvases (Embedded Mini-Simulations)
- **Current State**: Static CSS radial gradients with an icon.
- **10x Move**: Replace the preview with a real-time, zero-overhead WebGL micro-viewport on hover. Moving the cursor tilts the mini black hole in real time without leaving the page.
- **Why it's 10x**: Transforms a static showcase into a playable spacetime playground.

### 🎛️ 2. "Custom Preset Creator" & JSON Export/Import
- **Current State**: Hardcoded presets only.
- **10x Move**: Add an **"Author New Preset"** modal where users can tweak mass, spin, disk color, accretion speed, and custom names, then generate a shareable link or export a preset `.json` file.
- **Why it's 10x**: Empowers teachers, creators, and students to save and share their own custom cosmic configurations.

### 🎥 3. "Export 4K Wallpaper & Video Clip Generator"
- **Current State**: User must launch the studio to record.
- **10x Move**: Direct **"📸 4K Wallpaper"** and **"⚡ Quick 5s Clip"** buttons directly on every gallery card that renders in the background and downloads instantly.
- **Why it's 10x**: Eliminates friction for social media sharing, YouTube thumbnails, and desktop wallpapers.
