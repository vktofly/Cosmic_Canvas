# Product Positioning & Differentiation Strategy: Cosmic Canvas

| Competitor / Alternative | Positioning Angle | Target Audience | Key Gap Left Open |

| **Universe Sandbox** ($30 Steam Game) | Full N-body gravity simulator | Gamers / Desktop PC | High barrier: requires paid purchase, 4GB install, high-end GPU. |

## 2. 5 Strategic Positioning Angles

### Option 1 (The Interactive Astrophysics Studio) — **(Recommended)**
> *"Cosmic Canvas is the only zero-install, browser-native 3D physics engine designed for students, educators, and creators who want to touch, simulate, and export relativistic black hole phenomena in real-time."*
- **Rationale**: Direct focus on browser accessibility, interactive sliders, and viral video export.
- **Key Moats**: 60 FPS WebGL shaders, zero download friction, Web Audio sonification, instant WebM export.

### Option 2 (The Tactile Spacetime Playground)
> *"For curious minds who want to understand Einstein's equations, Cosmic Canvas is an interactive cosmic sandbox that translates abstract general relativity into touchable visual mechanics."*
- **Rationale**: Positions against dry math by highlighting physical intuition.

### Option 3 (The Creator's Cosmic Visualizer)
> *"The quickest way for science communicators and creators to generate high-fidelity, scientifically accurate relativistic motion graphics in 5 seconds."*
- **Rationale**: Focuses on video export and social sharing utility.

### Option 4 (The Mobile-First Quantum & Cosmic Observatory)
> *"Turn your smartphone or VR headset into a spatial observatory looking directly into an active event horizon."*
- **Rationale**: Leverages device orientation gyroscope and WebXR capabilities.

### Option 5 (The Classroom-Ready Relativity Lab)
> *"A free, zero-footprint virtual physics laboratory for university physics departments and schools."*
- **Rationale**: Institutional/educational positioning.
