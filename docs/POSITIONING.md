## 2. Active Product Positioning Strategy (Option 1: The Interactive Astrophysics Studio)

> **"Cosmic Canvas is the only zero-install, browser-native 3D physics engine designed for students, educators, and creators who want to touch, simulate, and export relativistic black hole phenomena in real-time."**

### Strategic Rationale:
- **Zero-Barrier Access**: Eliminates the 4GB download and high GPU hardware barrier of desktop software.
- **Creator & Education Multiplier**: Enables teachers and science creators to generate 60 FPS simulations and export viral clips in seconds.
- **Multi-Sensory Immersion**: Combines 3D WebGL shaders, spatial gyroscope VR tracking, and Web Audio acoustic synthesis into one cohesive studio.
