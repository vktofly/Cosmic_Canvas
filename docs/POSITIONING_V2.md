## 2. 5 Distinct Positioning Vectors

### Option 1: "The Zero-Barrier Relativistic Astrophysics Studio" (Current Anchor)
- **Positioning Statement**: *Cosmic Canvas is the only browser-native 3D general relativity studio designed for educators, researchers, and creators who want to simulate, pilot through, and export Kerr black hole phenomena in real-time with zero installation.*
- **Strategic Rationale**: Eliminates the 50GB barrier of SpaceEngine while providing actual relativistic physics missing in Universe Sandbox.
- **Key Advantage**: 60 FPS WebGL shader pipeline, WASD relativistic flight simulator, and 60 FPS video export directly in the browser.

### Option 2: "The AI-Powered Spacetime Planetarium"
- **Positioning Statement**: *Cosmic Canvas is the first interactive web planetarium with an autonomous AI voice narrator that guides students and curious minds through general relativity and cosmological extremes.*
- **Strategic Rationale**: Bridges the gap between static science videos and complex simulation tools with hands-free educational guidance.
- **Key Advantage**: Native Web Speech API tour integration synchronized with real-time parameter morphing.

### Option 3: "The Cinematic Spacetime Asset Studio for Science Creators"
- **Positioning Statement**: *Cosmic Canvas is the web's premier tool for astrophysics YouTubers and educators who need scientifically-grounded, 60 FPS 4K-ready video captures of black holes and wormholes.*
- **Strategic Rationale**: Video creators spend hours rendering Blender black holes; Cosmic Canvas allows instant custom parameters and one-click WebM/MP4 export.
- **Key Advantage**: Built-in 60 FPS HTML5 orbital capture pipeline with custom tilt and lensing adjustments.

### Option 4: "The Interstellar Spaceflight Sandbox"
- **Positioning Statement**: *Cosmic Canvas is the only browser-based relativistic flight simulator that lets you manually pilot a spacecraft at $0.99c$ into an event horizon with optical velocity aberration and acoustic gravitational redshift.*
- **Strategic Rationale**: Taps into the high-engagement gamer/sci-fi demographic inspired by *Interstellar*.
- **Key Advantage**: True relativistic Lorentz optical warping and Web Audio gravitational frequency shifts.

### Option 5: "The Interactive Cosmos Scale Playground"
- **Positioning Statement**: *Cosmic Canvas is an interactive 'Powers of Ten' cosmos explorer that grounds general relativity from 20 km neutron stars to 390-billion-km ultramassive black holes.*
- **Strategic Rationale**: Scale comparisons generate massive viral engagement across science communication channels.
- **Key Advantage**: Dynamic camera framing across 10 orders of astronomical magnitude in one unified viewport.
