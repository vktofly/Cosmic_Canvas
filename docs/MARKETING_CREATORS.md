**Product**: Cosmic Canvas — Spacetime Visualizer for Creators  
**Target Audience**: YouTube Video Essayists, Sci-Fi Streamers, Lo-Fi Music Producers, Science Communicators, TikTok/Reels Creators.

## 1. Top 5 High-Leverage Marketing Strategies

### 1. 🔥 "Powered by Cosmic Canvas" Watermark Viral Loop (#87, #93)
- **The Tactic**: Free video exports include a subtle, elegant semi-transparent watermark: `✦ Rendered on Cosmic Canvas`.
- **How to Start**:
  1. Embed default canvas watermark on all 60 FPS client recordings.
  2. Provide a 1-click button: *"Remove Watermark with Pro"* or *"Share on X to unlock 4K export"*.
- **Expected Outcome**: Hundreds of YouTube science videos and TikToks passively advertise the studio.

### 2. 🎵 Royalty-Free Lo-Fi & Sci-Fi Background Video Pack on Gumroad & GitHub (#15)
- **The Tactic**: Package 20 pre-rendered, seamless 4K 60 FPS looping space visualizers (Gargantua, Wormhole, Pulsar) as a **100% Free Creative Commons Asset Pack**.
- **How to Start**:
  1. Distribute pack on Gumroad, Reddit (`r/Filmmakers`, `r/videoediting`, `r/Streaming`), and GitHub.
  2. Include a README: *"Need custom orbits? Generate them live at Cosmic Canvas"*.
- **Expected Outcome**: Rapid top-of-funnel adoption among video editors who bookmark the studio.

### 3. 🎥 Short-Form "Black Hole Paradox" Series for TikTok, Shorts & Reels (#37)
- **The Tactic**: 30-second vertical videos demonstrating mind-bending relativistic phenomena (*"What happens if you fly a ship into Gargantua?"*, *"Why is one side of the black hole 10x brighter?"*).
- **How to Start**:
  1. Record 60 FPS vertical video clips directly using the Relativistic Flight Simulator and Voice Q&A.
  2. Add bold captions and end screen CTA: *"Test this black hole in your browser at the link in bio"*.
- **Expected Outcome**: 50k–500k viral views per video tapping into astronomical curiosity.

### 4. 🚀 Launch on Product Hunt & Hacker News Show HN (#78, #86)
- **The Tactic**: Dedicated Show HN: *"Show HN: Cosmic Canvas – Browser-native 60 FPS Kerr black hole simulator with Web Audio & Flight Mode"*.
- **How to Start**:
  1. Prepare live demo link, 30s WebM teaser GIF, and transparent engineering writeup.
  2. Launch mid-week (Tuesday/Wednesday 00:01 PST).
- **Expected Outcome**: #1 Product of the Day, front-page Hacker News, 20k+ instant visitors.

### 5. 🤝 YouTube Sci-Fi / Science Channel Sponsorships & Asset Kits (#54)
- **The Tactic**: Partner with mid-sized astrophysics YouTubers (e.g., Anton Petrov, Astrum, PBS Space Time community).
- **How to Start**:
