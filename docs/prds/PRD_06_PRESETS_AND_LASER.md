# Product Requirement Document (PRD): Celestial Presets & Drag-to-Aim Photon Laser Cannon

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Product**: Cosmic Canvas
- **Feature**: Real Astronomical Presets Library & Drag-to-Aim Photon Laser
- **Goal**: Expand interactivity by allowing users to explore real-world astronomical black holes (Sagittarius A*, Gargantua, Cygnus X-1) and interactively drag-aim photon trajectory beams.

---

## 2. Requirements & User Stories

### 2.1 Astronomical Presets Library
- **US-1**: As a space enthusiast, I want to click preset buttons for **Sagittarius A*** (Milky Way Supermassive), **Gargantua** (Interstellar Kerr Black Hole), and **Cygnus X-1** (Stellar Mass) to see real physical parameters automatically applied.

### 2.2 Drag-to-Aim Laser Cannon
- **US-2**: As an interactive user, I want to click and drag across the WebGL canvas to continuously cast photon trajectory lasers and watch them curve into relativistic photon orbits.
- **US-3**: Synthesize real-time polyphonic audio laser sweeps on each beam emitted.

---

## 3. Success Criteria
- Instant parameter morphing between astronomical presets.
- Click-drag generates fluid curved photon geodesic trajectories at 60 FPS.
