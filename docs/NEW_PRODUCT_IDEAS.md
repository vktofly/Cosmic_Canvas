# Cosmic Canvas: Green-Field Product Expansion & New Product Ideation

**Date**: 2026-08-14  
**Framework**: Blue Ocean Strategy & 0-to-1 Product Ideation  
**Scope**: New standalone products and adjacent market expansions built on the Cosmic Canvas WebGL/Astrophysics engine.

---

## 1. 🌌 "Cosmic Studio Pro" (WebXR / Apple Vision Pro Spatial Planetarium)
- **Concept**: A full WebXR spatial computing app where users walk inside the 3D photon sphere and feel the accretion disk spinning around their body in physical room space.
- **Target Market**: Spatial headset owners (Vision Pro, Meta Quest 3) and science museums.
- **Monetization**: $19.99 one-time purchase or institutional site license for science centers.

---

## 2. 🎮 "Relativity Run" (WebGL 6-DOF Spacetime Racing & Combat Game)
- **Concept**: A competitive physics-based multiplayer web game where players race light-speed spacecraft through wormholes, using relativistic gravitational slingshots ($GM/r$) around black holes to gain orbital velocity.
- **Target Market**: Casual gamers, sci-fi enthusiasts, Interstellar fans.
- **Key Mechanic**: Real-time gravitational time dilation changes your ship's clock vs opponents.

---

## 3. 🧩 "Astrophysics Canvas SDK" (Embeddable Three.js Astrophysics NPM Library)
- **Concept**: Package the core relativistic engine (`@cosmic-canvas/engine`) as an open-source/commercial NPM module for edtech companies, university websites, and scientific publishers (NASA, ESA, Nature).
- **Target Market**: Frontend developers building scientific visualizers.
- **Value Proposition**: 1-line integration `<black-hole mass="100M" spin="0.998" />`.

---

## 4. 🎬 "Spacetime Visualizer for Creators" (Automated Sci-Fi Background Video Generator)
- **Concept**: Web app for YouTube creators, podcasters, and musicians to generate 4K looping cosmic backdrop animations with custom audio-reactive accretion disks and galaxy nebulae.
- **Target Market**: Lo-Fi music creators, YouTube video essayists, sci-fi streamers.
- **Monetization**: Freemium (720p free, 4K/Pro render credits).

---

## 5. 🪐 "Universe Sandbox Web" (N-Body Solar System Builder & Galaxy Collisions)
- **Concept**: A browser-native solar system creation sandbox allowing users to place stars, planets, and moons, then introduce a wandering rogue black hole to watch the gravitational chaos.
- **Target Market**: K-12 STEM classrooms, university astronomy courses.
