Astrophysics, Mathematics & WebGL Architecture

A transparent technical reference for the relativistic equations, numerical approximations, and graphics pipeline behind Cosmic Canvas.

1. The Kerr Metric & Horizon Geodesics

For a rotating uncharged black hole with mass \(M\) and angular momentum parameter \(a = J / M\), the spacetime interval in Boyer-Lindquist coordinates is given by:

ds² = -(1 - 2Mr/Σ) dt² - (4Mar sin²θ / Σ) dt dφ + (Σ / Δ) dr² + Σ dθ² + (r² + a² + 2Ma²r sin²θ / Σ) sin²θ dφ²

where: Σ = r² + a² cos²θ, Δ = r² - 2Mr + a²

The event horizon outer boundary \(r_+\) and static limit (ergosphere) \(r_{\text{ergo}}\) are calculated as:

r₊ = M + √(M² - a²)
r_ergo(θ) = M + √(M² - a² cos²θ)

2. Relativistic Doppler Beaming & Optical Aberration

Plasma in the accretion disk orbiting at relativistic speeds \(\vec{v}\) exhibits asymmetric radiant flux towards an observer according to the relativistic Doppler factor \(D\):

D = 1 / (γ (1 - β cos θ))
I_obs(ν) = D⁴ · I_emit(ν / D)

The \(D^4\) radiance factor produces the characteristic visual asymmetry where the plasma moving towards the camera appears exponentially brighter and blue-shifted, while receding material is dimmed and red-shifted.

3. WebGL Engine & Web Audio Synthesis Graph

Cosmic Canvas executes entirely on the client GPU and Web Audio thread:

  • Post-Processing: Three.js EffectComposer with downsampled UnrealBloomPass and ACESFilmicToneMapping ($<0.8\text{ms}$ GPU frametime).
  • Zero-GC Vector Pools: Pre-allocated scratchpad buffers for $O(N^2)$ N-body gravity calculations, completely avoiding Garbage Collection stutter.
  • Gravitational Redshift Sonar: Real-time Web Audio synthesis modulated by the gravitational time dilation factor \(\sqrt{1 - R_s / r}\).
