Privacy Policy & Client Data Architecture

Cosmic Canvas is designed from the ground up as a 100% private, client-side, local-computation astrophysics platform.

1. Microphone & Web Speech Recognition Data

When you click "🎤 Ask Voice", your browser's built-in Web Speech API is invoked exclusively to convert spoken astrophysics queries into simulation commands.

Zero Audio Retention: Audio is processed directly on your device. We do not record, store, transmit, or monetize your voice or microphone streams under any circumstances.

2. Local GPU Computation & Analytics

All Three.js WebGL 2.0 rendering, N-body gravitational simulations, and Web Audio synthesis run entirely on your local GPU and CPU. No simulation telemetry is transferred to remote servers.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Cookies

Cosmic Canvas does not use third-party advertising cookies, user profiling trackers, or invasive analytics pixels. Your session belongs entirely to you.
